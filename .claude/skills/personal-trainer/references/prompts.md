# The Prompt Stack

Ready-made prompts the athlete can fire at the coach. These are conveniences — the
coach responds to natural language too. (Adapted from the original Hawks guides.)

## Setup

**First-time setup**
```
Read my profile, exercise library, lift log, and nutrition file.
Acknowledge what you know about me so far and flag anything missing
before we start building my programme and targets.
```

## Training prompts

**Build your programme**
```
Based on my profile, goals, and exercise library, build me a full 4 day
training programme. Use block periodisation — 4 weeks building volume,
4 weeks increasing intensity, 3 weeks going heavy, 1 week deload.
Give me Day 1 in full with exercises, sets, reps, working weights and RPE
targets. Then outline the structure for Days 2, 3 and 4.
```

**Beginner starting point**
```
I'm new to the gym and have no established working weights yet. Build me a
beginner-friendly 4 day programme, start everything conservatively, and use
Week 1 to find my working weights. Walk me through what to expect in the
first month.
```

**Swap an exercise**
```
I want to swap out [exercise] from my programme. Based on my exercise
library, what's the best replacement that hits the same muscle group and
fits my current block? Update the programme going forward.
```

**Deload week**
```
I need a deload this week. Based on my current programme, give me a full
deload week — same exercises but adjusted volume and intensity. I want to
recover properly without losing momentum.
```

**Plateau buster**
```
My [lift] has been stuck at [weight] for [X] sessions. Look at my log and
tell me why you think it's stalled, then give me a 3 week plan to break
through it.
```

**Log a session** (just paste it — the coach appends it and updates weights)
```
Log today's session:
[DD/MM/YYYY] — Push Day
Barbell Bench Press | 4 x 6 | 80 | 8 | felt strong
Incline DB Press    | 3 x 10 | 28 | 7 |
Lateral Raise       | 3 x 15 | 10 | 8 |
Session notes: good energy, slept 8h
```

## Nutrition prompts

**Calculate your targets**
```
Based on my stats and goals, calculate my daily calorie target for a
sustainable fat loss deficit. Also give me my daily protein target.
Show your working — I want to understand the numbers, not just follow
them blindly.
```

**Build your weekly meal plan**
```
Using my food preferences, build me a 7-day meal plan that hits my calorie
and protein targets. Use foods I actually eat. Keep it simple — I'm not
spending an hour cooking every night. Include rough macro breakdowns per day.
```

**Handle a problem meal**
```
I struggle to hit my protein target at [breakfast/lunch/dinner]. Based on the
foods I eat, give me 5 options for that meal that are high protein, under [X]
calories, and take under [Y] minutes to make.
```

**Stalled progress**
```
My weight hasn't moved in [X] weeks. I've been eating to my targets most days.
Walk me through the most likely reasons and tell me what to try first before
I cut calories further.
```

**Eat out without stress**
```
I'm eating out at [restaurant type] on Saturday. Give me a strategy for
ordering smart, rough calorie estimates for common dishes, and how to adjust
my eating earlier in the day to account for it.
```
