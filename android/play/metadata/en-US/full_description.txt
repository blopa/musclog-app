<b>Musclog - Lift, Log, Repeat</b>

Musclog logs your lifts and your food in one app. No account, no ads, and your data stays on your phone. Free and open source.

<b>Train</b>
• Workout plans, schedules, reminders and rest timers, with plate math for your gym's bar and plates.
• Progression that explains every number it changes, plus a Weekly Review of your training and nutrition.
• Supersets, warm-ups, exercise notes and per-exercise units.
• Hundreds of exercises with photos, searchable in your language.
• Import your history from Strong, Hevy or FitNotes.
• Strength levels, personal records and recent training load per muscle.
• Comeback mode: an easier restart if you have been away.

<b>Eat</b>
• Food diary with barcode and nutrition label scanning.
• Search Open Food Facts and the USDA database, or create your own foods and meals.
• Calorie and macro targets that adapt to your real weight trend, in kcal or kJ.

<b>Know your body</b>
• Weight, body fat, measurements and mood, with charts.
• Optional menstrual cycle tracking: log periods and symptoms and see your own patterns. Predictions are estimates, and there is no phase-based training advice.
• Health Connect sync for weight, nutrition, workouts and body metrics.

<b>AI, only if you want it</b>
• Chat with a coach, analyse meal photos and generate plans using your own Gemini, OpenAI or Claude key, a local model, or the free option built into the app.
• Choose how the coach talks to you. Nothing in Musclog requires AI.

<b>Yours</b>
• Weight, body fat and food logs are encrypted on your device. Everything works offline.
• Export or back up your data, or move it to another phone by scanning QR codes, with no cloud.
• Anonymous crash reports help fix bugs and can be turned off in settings.
• Home screen widgets, several color themes, and English, Español, Nederlands, Português (Brasil) and Русский.
