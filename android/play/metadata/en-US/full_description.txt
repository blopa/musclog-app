<b>Musclog - Lift, Log, Repeat</b>

Musclog is a workout tracker and calorie counter in one free, open source app. Log your gym sessions and your food, follow your progress, and keep it all on your phone: no account, no ads, and logging works offline.

<b>Workout log and gym tracker</b>
• Log sets, reps and weight in seconds, with a rest timer, warm-up sets, supersets and notes for every exercise.
• Track effort with RPE or reps in reserve (RIR).
• 870+ exercises with photos and target muscles, searchable in your language, or add your own.
• Ready-made workout plans: Push Pull Legs, Upper/Lower, Full Body, PHUL, 5-day splits and short home or no-equipment routines. Or build your own weekly or rotating program.
• Progressive overload made simple: Musclog suggests your next weight and reps from your last session and explains every number it changes. Pick double progression, linear or percentage-based rules, or keep full control.
• Plate calculator for your gym's bar and plates, in kg or lb, per exercise if you like.
• Swap an exercise when a machine is busy or something hurts, and let Musclog remember what to avoid.
• Import your history from Strong, Hevy or FitNotes.

<b>See your strength grow</b>
• Personal records, estimated 1RM and strength levels for the main lifts.
• Recent training load per muscle on a body map, and a calendar heatmap of your workouts.
• A Weekly Review of your training and nutrition, where you choose to progress, repeat the week or go lighter.
• Comeback mode: an easier restart after a break.

<b>Calorie counter and macro tracker</b>
• Food diary with a barcode scanner and nutrition label scanning.
• Search Open Food Facts and the USDA food database, or create your own foods and meals.
• Calorie, protein, carb, fat and fiber targets for fat loss, maintenance or muscle gain.
• Targets that adapt to your real weight trend, in kcal or kJ, metric or imperial.

<b>Body tracking</b>
• Weight, body fat, body measurements and mood, with charts.
• Optional menstrual cycle tracking: log periods and symptoms and see your own patterns. Predictions are estimates, and there is no phase-based training advice.
• Health Connect sync for weight, nutrition, workouts and body metrics.

<b>AI, only if you want it</b>
• Chat with a fitness coach, estimate a meal from a photo and generate workout plans using your own Gemini, OpenAI or Claude key, a local model, or the free option built into the app.
• Or build a plan or describe a meal in ChatGPT or Claude and bring it into Musclog.
• Choose how the coach talks to you. Nothing in Musclog requires AI.

<b>Private by design</b>
• Weight, body fat and food logs are encrypted on your device.
• Lock the whole app, or only your cycle, workout, food or progress data, with your phone's fingerprint or PIN.
• Export or back up your data, or move it to another phone by scanning QR codes, with no cloud.
• Anonymous crash reports help fix bugs and can be turned off in settings.
• Home screen widgets, several color themes, and English, Español, Nederlands, Português (Brasil) and Русский.
