The upper lower split trains your upper body on two days and your lower body on two days. If you have a job and can train four days a week, it is a practical way to hit every muscle twice without spending five or six days in the gym. Musclog has the whole program written out, with every exercise, set and rep, and you can open it in the app with one tap from the [Upper/Lower 4-Day Split program page](/programs/upper-lower-4-day).

## The four workouts

The program has four workouts: two upper-body days and two lower-body days. Each one is written to take about 65 minutes according to the program file, and each has three sets per exercise.

**Upper A (day 1)**

| Exercise                          | Sets × reps |
| --------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Barbell Bench Press - Medium Grip | 3 × 10      |
| Barbell Shoulder Press            | 3 × 10      |
| Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown            | 3 × 12      |
| Seated Cable Rows                 | 3 × 12      |
| Side Lateral Raise                | 3 × 15      |
| Dumbbell Bicep Curl               | 3 × 12      |
| Triceps Pushdown                  | 3 × 12      |

**Lower A (day 2)**

| Exercise                    | Sets × reps |
| --------------------------- | ----------- |
| Barbell Squat               | 3 × 10      |
| Leg Press                   | 3 × 12      |
| Romanian Deadlift           | 3 × 12      |
| Lying Leg Curls             | 3 × 12      |
| Leg Extensions              | 3 × 12      |
| Rocking Standing Calf Raise | 3 × 15      |

**Upper B (day 3)**

| Exercise                                  | Sets × reps |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Barbell Incline Bench Press - Medium Grip | 3 × 12      |
| Incline Dumbbell Press                    | 3 × 12      |
| Pullups                                   | 3 × 10      |
| Leverage Iso Row                          | 3 × 10      |
| Face Pull                                 | 3 × 15      |
| Hammer Curls                              | 3 × 12      |
| Seated Triceps Press                      | 3 × 12      |

**Lower B (day 4)**

| Exercise                    | Sets × reps |
| --------------------------- | ----------- |
| Smith Machine Squat         | 3 × 10      |
| Hack Squat                  | 3 × 12      |
| Romanian Deadlift           | 3 × 12      |
| Dumbbell Shrug              | 3 × 12      |
| Standing Barbell Calf Raise | 3 × 15      |

Upper A and Upper B use different chest and back exercises, and the lower days use different squat variations. The Romanian deadlift appears on both lower days.

## What each muscle gets each week

Counting the four workouts, each muscle group is trained twice a week. Chest gets the bench press on day 1 and the incline bench on day 3. Back gets the lat pulldown and cable row on day 1, and the pull-up and iso row on day 3. Quads get squats on both lower days, and calves get raises on both lower days. Biceps and triceps each get two exercises across the week.

This is what the split does: it spreads the work so no muscle waits a full week between sessions. Whether that is the best frequency for you is a question for your own results, not something this post can settle.

## A schedule for a busy week

The program does not fix weekdays, so pick the ones that fit your job. Four sessions with a gap between them works well:

| Day      | Workout |
| -------- | ------- |
| Monday   | Upper A |
| Tuesday  | Lower A |
| Thursday | Upper B |
| Friday   | Lower B |

The two-day gap on the weekend gives you time to rest before the cycle starts again. Musclog lets you choose the weekdays when you open the program, so you can move the days around.

## When you miss a day

Life with a job means some sessions will slip. The program page says to do the workouts in order and repeat the cycle. The order matters more than the weekday. If you miss Tuesday's lower session, do Lower A on your next free day, then carry on with Upper B. You do not need to double up or skip ahead.

## Starting weights and progression

Start with weights you can lift with good form and a couple of reps left. Musclog suggests starting weights from your profile when you open the program.

The program page gives one simple rule of thumb: when you reach the top of the rep range on every set, add weight next time. It is a rule of thumb, not a research result. The file lists one rep target per exercise, so a practical version is to add weight once you hit the target on every set.

## Who this split suits

This split suits someone who can train four times a week for about an hour, and who wants each muscle trained twice without a six-day schedule. If you can only train three days, the [Push Pull Legs program](/blog/2026/11/push-pull-legs-a-free-program-you-can-open-in-your-app) runs on three workouts. If you have an injury or a health condition, check with a doctor or physiotherapist before you start.
